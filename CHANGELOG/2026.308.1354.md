# 2026.308.1354 — feat(design): restore the numeric matchers and the command's own fields (batch 19)

## Summary

Batch 19 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). **Two rows: one
restored whole, one restored in part with its remainder recorded as a narrowing.**

- **`verb:command` becomes `sharp`.** `expect_non_zero` (the command must *fail* — the assertion that
  turns a "does it reject this?" test into a check) and `skip_exit` (the "ran nothing" code) join
  `#CheckFields`, and `background` joins `#RunFields`.
- **`verb:matching` restores its numeric comparisons** — `#MatcherAlt` gains `lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge` —
  while the **multi-matcher list stays out**: D-VERB-3 states that a matcher is exactly one comparison,
  and `not`/`json` compose any matcher. That narrowing joins the other three in A.9, and the row cites
  it.

The examples had **no run step at all**, so the two added ones (a guarded directory creation and a
background key refresh) are also the design's first.
