# 2026.326.2118 — feat(umbrella)!: remove the retired pod-immich-ml submodule

## Summary

Removes the retired `pod-immich-ml` submodule from the umbrella. The Immich ML backend
has lived in the single opencharly/pod-immich image since its v3.4.1 fold (now v3.4.2,
tag `v2026.326.1824`), and `pod-immich-ml` has been a documentation-only no-op awaiting
retirement (class-wide tracker opencharly/opencharly#600).

- `.gitmodules`: the `[submodule "pod-immich-ml"]` block is deleted and the gitlink is
  removed from the index.
- `README.md`: the org-map row is deleted, the “Pods: AI and ML services” section moves
  from 16 to 15 repos, and the header census moves from 399 to 398.
- `.agents/skills/agents/references/parallel-bed-testing.md`: the stale bed name
  `check-cachyos-immich-ml-pod` becomes `check-cachyos-immich-pod`.

The `pod-immich` pin itself is LEFT where the index records it. Advancing pins is the
org-wide `charly task sync` wave's job, never a hand-pin; `verify` reports the pin as
behind, which passes by design and is recorded here as the currency observation.
