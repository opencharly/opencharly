2. **opencharly/plugin-task#12 — the task reporter drops a failing nested step's message.**

   `task "pins" failed (1 step(s) failed)` with no path and no reason. Seconds-long minimal
   reproduction in the issue, plus the measured note that `--json` does not bypass it.
3. **opencharly/plugin-task#11 — `bump` advances a submodule's gitlink but leaves that
   submodule's own INITIALIZED nested checkouts stale.** Advancing `charly` moves `charly`'s own
   `box/*` gitlinks; `pinSubmodule` (`verbs.go:318`) switches the superproject's pointer only, so
   the shared tree reports those paths modified and `task verify` fails on a change the sync itself
   made.

**Unblock order.** #12 is the prerequisite for ever naming a `pins`-step failure from the `sync`
entrypoint; #13 is what keeps one path's contention from costing the whole run; #11 is
