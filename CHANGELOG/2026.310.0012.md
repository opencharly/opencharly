## Summary

Batch 51 of the DESIGN amendments (tracked in #838). Three rows asked for operations **their own dulling text
already rules out**, and each names the rule:

| row | the rule that forbids it |
|---|---|
| `cache status\|clear\|refresh\|bypass\|push\|pull\|ingest` | **D-STORE-3** — *one content-addressed store holds every cached item … and no other on-disk cache*. The verbs act on the store; a second disk cache has nothing to manage. |

| `candy set\|params` | a **`parameter:`** field is a declaration, and **D-CLI-2** already names what may write, so nothing has to regenerate a value afterwards. |
