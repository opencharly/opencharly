# 2026.308.0236 — feat(design): name the compositor's surfaces as concepts (batch 4b)

## Summary

Batch 4b of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). Batch 4a restored
the display check's generic twelve arms; this adds the two observations authored documents make of
the **compositor itself**.

`hypr-layers` is the most-used display operation in the tree — **97 authored uses** — and it reports
a layer-shell surface present, sized and on screen. The design cannot call it that: `layer` and
`workspace` are both §2 **Not** terms (a build layer; an old name for a project). So the arms are
named as **concepts**:

- `surface` — a layer-shell surface: a bar, a background, an overlay, a launcher;
- `screen` — an output.

Each carries a name, presence and a size. The mapping is recorded in the ledger rather than implied:
`hypr-layers` → `surface`, `hypr-monitors` → `screen`.

**What remains is the compositor's own verb set** — `sway-*`, `hypr-dispatch`, `hypr-eval`,
`hypr-systeminfo`, `overlay-*`, `atspi`, `ocr`, `xprop`, `geometry`, `toplevel`, `fullscreen`,
`resolution`, `scroll`, `drag`, `double-click`, `key-combo` — and the `wl` row now says so with its
count (32 of 46) instead of the previous 34.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN.cue` | `surface` and `screen` arms with their two definitions |
| `DESIGN.md` | the display example asserts a bar's surface at a known size and the primary output's dimensions |
| `design/dulling.tsv` | the `wl` row's remainder and the concept mapping |
