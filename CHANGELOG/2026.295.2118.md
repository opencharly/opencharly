## Summary

An audit of DESIGN.* after #597 and #598. It covers three things: rules stated once, a schema that applies its own patterns uniformly, and check machinery that describes and self-tests every control it runs. There are no shoehorned exceptions; each fix removes a special case rather than adding one.

**Each rule is stated in exactly one place** (DESIGN.md's own contract). These pairs restated one rule, and now one of each pair states it and the other cites it:
- D-VERB-1 and D-VERB-6: verb classes and application checks;
- D-KIND-1, D-PLUG-7 and D-VAL-10: namespaced foreign words;
- D-CLI-1 and D-CLI-3: application commands;
- D-HW-7 and D-VENUE-2: a microVM venue has no `exec`;
- D-DRV-2 and D-HW-3: an unmet need fails early;
- D-CAPTURE-4 and D-VAL-11: process capture;
- D-CANDY-6 and D-STORE-9: `local-install`;
- D-KIND-5 and S10.

§7.1 restated D-CLI-2's list of writers and contradicted it: `box reconcile` and `deploy adopt` were filed as driver commands, while `box cat` and `box merge` sat in the editing row. Its three core-command rows are now one row that cites D-CLI-2.

**The schema applies its own patterns, without exception:**
- **One authored form per package.** `package:` is always a set of locators, and a manager's input is `package_input:` keyed by its locator. The old string-or-struct union was the never-table's "scalar shorthand for a struct". New §5.4 rule **D-VAL-13**, in rules.cue: every input names a listed package.
- **S5 sets.** Every set is a unique list (`#TextSet`, `#RepoSet`, `#TypeNameSet`), including a manifest's `provide:` role words. Published ports are written as a map named after the `provide: port` they publish, e.g. `port: {http: {host: 18080, guest: 8080}}`. That pairing is a naming convention in this PR: no rule checks a key against `provide:`. The follow-up schema review turns it into a checked §5.4 rule.
- **S6.** The `gpu` and `mount` needs embed the one `#Optional` fragment.
- **S3.** Repeated enums are word lists: `_scopeWord`, `_busWord` and `_formWord`. The `_`-typed `_directive` struct is now `_directiveWord`, which LIST_PAIRS still checks against `#Document`.
- **S2.** The audit timestamp is a named scalar, `#Timestamp`.
- **Naming.** Each single-verb input is named `#<Verb>Check`, and its operation table `#<Verb>CheckAlt`. `vnc` and `spice` share `#ScreenCheck`, because they are one observation over two transports.
- **S4.** `#Builder` is a tag table, `bootstrap` or `manager`; the old all-optional form could not say what a builder does. The old `output?` field is dropped: no document or example ever used it (evidence below).
- **Removed.**
  - the dead `#Manager` and `#Role` (D-LAW-2);
  - the `need` that `#AgentBody` re-declared;
  - the redundant disjunction `phase: "runtime" | *"runtime"`;
  - the single-use `#SourceNode` and `#OsNode` wrappers.

**Check machinery:**
- A.4 and the consistency script's header now describe all seven checks; check 7, table shape, was undocumented. The headers name the verb-table control and the `user-config` example mode.
- **A §5.4 negative now isolates its rule.** `design-examples.sh` requires every D-VAL negative to pass `cue vet -d '#Document'` before rules.cue judges it, and A.5 states this.
- Four new self-test rows: a dangling § in the ledger's `home` column, a table row without its trailing `|`, a protocol negative that is wrongly accepted, and a D-VAL negative that also fails the schema.
- Six new negative cases: the three reference rules #597 added (roster `bed`, stage `pipeline`, builder `box`), plus D-VAL-13, D-IR-6, and the builder's one-arm rule (D-PLUG-2). `builder-box-candy` now names `manager: npm`, so it fails only on its reference.
- Two negative cases were re-targeted at the new forms: `type-name-hyphen` and `manager-attributes-as-core-fields`.
