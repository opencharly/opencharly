- **`fail_on`** — the outcome names that count as a failure; an outcome not named is a pass.
- **`redo`** (`#Redo`) — the outcomes that are re-run, **how many times**, and **after how many failures
  the run escalates** instead of repeating itself. That last field is the one that keeps a flaky bed from
  burning the whole budget silently.

The two sibling D-VERB-5 rows **stay dulled**, and both are swept because this change falsifies what they
say about the definition it grows:

- **`verb:agent`** — the step's own `redo` is now carried, so **9 of 10 becomes 8 of 10**; the row names
  the new contents of `#HarnessStepFields`.
- **`verb:probe`** — `redo` is carried and `media` is not, so **4 of 5 becomes 3 of 5**.

Both cite `#Redo` in their evidence, which the ledger control requires of every definition a row names.
