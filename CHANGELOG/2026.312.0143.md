## Summary

Batch 67 of the DESIGN amendments (tracked in #838). `cua:` is a device entity carrying a driver binary, a host, a
permission mode with a danger acknowledgement, a capability manifest and a read-only control gate.

Its dulling cell said `#Harness` was `{argv, model, need, use}` — true when written, **false since**: `argv` *is*
the driver binary, and the host is the deployment's venue. The manifest needed a home and did not have one: batch 56
put a `tool` list on the **step** (`#HarnessStepFields.tool`), not on the node.

**So this PR makes two schema additions:**

1. **`tool?: [...#Text]` on `#Harness`** — the node-level default a step narrows, exactly as it narrows `llm`.
2. **`permission` on `#Harness`** — one word of a closed set, using **the plugin's own words**:

| word | what it means |
|---|---|
| `standard` | observes only; the control gate is shut — and it is the **default** |
| `bounded` | acts inside what its `tool` list allows |
| `unrestricted` | the one that needs the acknowledgement |

`kind:cua` becomes **sharp**.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN.cue` | `_permission` + `_permissionWord`; `tool?` and `permission` added to `#Harness` |
| `DESIGN.md` | the review-harness example declares `permission: bounded` |
| `design/dulling.tsv` | `kind:cua` becomes sharp |
| `design/dulling-disposition.md` | regenerated |
