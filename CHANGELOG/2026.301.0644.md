## Summary

Head `e9376b42b3e139dafabf2ddb1042c51a69fb8bab` — the commit this PR pushes: **11 files changed, 527 insertions(+), 24 deletions(-)**

**The defect.** The umbrella's guidance introduced `charly task sync` as a thing that changed nothing. The manifest's task description read `Bump all submodule pins per the pin contract (preview; does not commit)`, and the README's maintenance row read `bump pins per the pin contract (preview; does not commit)`. The PERMISSION was legible — "advance pins only with `charly task sync`" — but the SCOPE was not, so the verb read like a targeted tool and turned out to be an org-wide wave over every pin the repo declares: it advances EVERY declared pin, rolls the checkouts that exist to their remote's default-branch HEAD, and stages their gitlinks. The half that was true was "does not commit"; the half that was false was "preview".

**The RCA.** *Mechanism* — the wording made a mutating org-wide cutover look like a no-op, and every surface that names a verb is prose, so nothing checked what those surfaces claimed. *Missed control* — no control asserted that a surface naming the pin-advance verb states what the verb advances: the retired-claim sweep had a class for the "preview" form but no positive counterpart, so a surface could state the scope wrongly, or not at all, and the tree stayed green. *Blast radius* — every operator surface: the manifest, the README, the rulebook, the `git-workflow` skill and three of its references, the `agents` skill, the `repo-setup` checklist. *Root fix* — a CONTROL, not a lesson: the retired-claim key set gains the class, and a new gate asserts the positive claim.

**What this change does.**

- The retired wording is deleted from every surface that carried it — the README's maintenance row, and the manifest's header comment and task description.
- Every surface that presents the pin-advance verb now states its scope: 18 claim-maker surfaces across 9 files, each saying the verb advances EVERY pin the repo declares, and that it does not commit and does not open a PR.
- Two controls keep the claim true, and they do not overlap. `scripts/check-retired-claims.mjs` gains the NEGATIVE class (the advance described as a non-mutating kind — a preview, a dry run, a read-only report). The new `scripts/check-pin-verb-scope.mjs` asserts the POSITIVE claim: every claim-maker surface states the scope, every enumerated surface exists at a unique anchor, and every tracked file naming the verb is classified. The new gate holds no "preview", "dry run" or "read-only" literal at all, so it cannot trip the class it checks.
- The coverage arm earned its place: it found a real gap the prose cutover had missed — `.agents/skills/repo-setup/SKILL.md` named the verb with no scope.
