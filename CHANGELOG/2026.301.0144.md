## Summary

Head `ab0cb873b5ea79e3a6fabb6434d4948b7b272271` — the commit this PR pushes: **14 files changed, 164 insertions(+), 59 deletions(-)**.

**The measured-figures control excused a count whenever the BLOCK that stated it carried a date, and one dated sentence excused every count the block held.** The unit a date reached was the block — a paragraph, a run of comment lines, an indent scope — so the exemption was a property of the BLOCK, not of the count it excused: a day that measured one thing silently excused the bare counts beside it, and nothing in the tree could tell the two apart. That is the control's own blind spot, and this change closes it.

**The unit a date reaches is now the CLAUSE.** A clause is the text between a sentence-ending mark and the colons, semicolons and commas that follow whitespace, and a count is excused when its OWN clause carries a date. A sentence-ending mark ends a sentence only when it ends one — the mark in "3.5" or in "charly.yml" is followed by no whitespace, and a comma followed by no whitespace is a thousands separator, not a clause boundary — and an em dash does not break a clause, which is what binds a count to the day it was measured with.

**The sites the rule un-dates move with it, in the same change.** Under the new rule the counts whose date belonged to another clause are reported: 24 of them, across 14 files, re-measured on the merged tree. Every one of those sites now carries its date in the count's own clause, or is restated in the control's own bound form ("at most 5000 calls/hr", a policy number and not a measurement), or — in `reasonix.toml` — as the derivation itself: the figures go, the derivation stays, and the moving subject ("the corpus this branch pins") and the restated breakdown go with them.

**The control's own self-test carries the discriminating arms.** A count whose own clause carries no date is reported although a LATER clause of the same block carries one, and although an EARLIER clause carries one; the dated-count arm takes the clause-scoped form, so the exemption is held by the clause and not by the block.
