# 2026.286.1143 — feat(umbrella): register the two OpenClaw upstream forks as submodules (#472)

## Summary

Registers the org's two OpenClaw upstream forks as umbrella submodules — after fast-forwarding
BOTH to their upstreams, because a pin must be the repo's own default-branch HEAD and both were
stale mirrors.

| fork | before | after | divergence at sync time |
|---|---|---|---|
| `opencharly/openclaw` | `f3af0db8` | `9354a2ca` | 2005 commits behind upstream, `0` fork-local |
| `opencharly/openclaw-enterprise` | `ce6535a7` | `3d180654` | 900 commits behind upstream, `0` fork-local |

- `.gitmodules` — two entries, each `branch = main`, the remote default that `task verify` audits.
- the gitlinks — `openclaw` at `9354a2ca`, `openclaw-enterprise` at `3d180654`.
- `README.md` — both rows in **Product & tooling**, that section `7 repos` -> `9 repos`, and the
  header `422 today` -> `424 today`.

**The unblock, and why it is in this body.** `opencharly/openclaw` could not be fast-forwarded
while the org ruleset still targeted it: it was created `2026-10-10T21:56:07Z`, after the last
`org-ruleset.sh apply`, so it had never entered the ruleset's computed `repository_name.exclude`
(forks are excluded by design — `scripts/org-ruleset.sh` computes that set, it is never declared).
`gh repo sync` therefore returned rc=1 with the head unchanged, while its sibling
`openclaw-enterprise` (created `2026-10-09T05:51:15Z`, already excluded) synced on the first try.
Adding the single name `openclaw` to ruleset `23827016`'s exclude list is a security-machinery
change, made at the operator's explicit direction and recorded on opencharly/.github#179 BEFORE
the edit; the live result is the ruleset fence below.

**Not materialized, deliberately, with the cost stated.** `opencharly/openclaw`'s history is
8.6 GB, while its `main` tree is 625 MB across 53 384 blobs (`git/trees?recursive=1`), so
virtually all of it is history. The umbrella records gitlinks and `verb:git-submodules
{mode: verify}` audits a pin without a checkout, so that 8.6 GB is paid by whoever first runs
`git submodule update --init openclaw` — not by every session that touches this repo or by every
fresh worktree. `openclaw-enterprise` (72 MB) is materialized and audited as a clean checkout.
