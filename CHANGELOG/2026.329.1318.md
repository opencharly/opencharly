# 2026.329.1318 — docs(agents): a spike round can be delegated, one candidate per worker

## Summary

The agents skill gains a short section on **delegating an R12 spike round**. R12 (opencharly/opencharly#1283) spikes every plausible solution for a named cause, and the candidates are independent of each other, so a parent may hand each one to its own worker. The section says:

- what the brief names: the cause, the one candidate, and the instrument;
- what the worker returns: its commands, outputs and exit codes verbatim, plus a works / fails result, never a summary;
- that the parent picks the winner and owns the `### Spike` block.

It points at strict-policy's "Spike-first" for the round itself, and restates none of it. This is the last item of the opencharly/opencharly#1278 plan.
