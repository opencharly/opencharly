wave landed at 02:09:48Z and advanced `charly` and the two `distro-*` edges to the very
SHAs this wave had advanced them to, so three of this wave's first six paths were
superseded before the push. On the fresh base those three are `cur`, so they drop out of
the derived scope, and the advance stays idempotent with respect to what has already
landed. The RCA — mechanism, missed control, blast radius and root fix — is on
**opencharly/opencharly#650**.

gitlinks with the product's own `git submodule update` — nothing was discarded — and the
wave re-ran from that clean state.

### What the wave is not

- Not a hand-pin, and not a branch checkout to "catch up": every pin moved through
  `charly task sync`.
- Not a new pin and not a new submodule: every path in scope is already declared in
  `.gitmodules`.
- Not a drift-everything bump: the paths the verb moved outside the scope were restored to
  their base gitlinks.
- Not `charly task init --all`: the wave materializes only the paths the verb needs.
- Not a re-pin of what #651 already advanced: a path another wave already advanced is
  `cur` on this base, and `cur` is out of the derived scope.
