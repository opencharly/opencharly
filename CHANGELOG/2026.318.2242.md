## Summary

The Omarchy family is retired: its **17 live repos are archived** in the org, and the umbrella
stops **pinning** them. Tracked as #1029.

Closes #1029.

ONE cutover moves all four umbrella surfaces together, so the index, `.gitmodules` and
`README.md` cannot disagree in between:

| surface | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| the index's mode-160000 entries | 409 | 392 |
| `.gitmodules` declared paths | 409 | 392 |
| the `pins:` task's `pin_map` edges | 6 | 5 |
| `README.md`'s org map (header census, and rows) | 409 | 392 |

- **the 17 pins** — dropped from the index, so no declaration outlives its pin.
- **`.gitmodules`** — the 17 `[submodule "…"]` sections (68 lines).
- **`charly.yml`** — the `distro-omarchy: box/omarchy` line of the `pins:` task's `pin_map`.
  That map is the pin contract's EDGE set keyed by UMBRELLA PATH, so the edge lost its
  referent the moment the path went; the other five distro edges stay.
- **the design census** — `design/parity.tsv` and `design/dulling.tsv` drop the retired
  `verb:omarchy` capability, and `design/dulling-disposition.md` is REGENERATED from the
  ledger (its `263 capabilities` line is the generated count, never a hand-computed one).
- **`README.md`** — the `distro-omarchy/` row (`### Distro image families` 6 → 5), the whole
  `### Omarchy layers` (13 repos) and `### Omarchy evaluation` (2 repos) sections, the
  `plugin-omarchy/` row (`### Plugins` 120 → 119), and the header census 409 → 392.

The five remaining distro edges — `arch`, `cachyos`, `debian`, `fedora`, `ubuntu` — still
hold; the `verify:` line under "How tested" is that verdict.

**What this diff does NOT touch, and why.** `charly` still imports `distro-omarchy` and pins
`box/omarchy`, and that is charly's own tree — Part II #1 forbids the umbrella from editing
inside a submodule. It is filed as opencharly/charly#1145 together with the measurement that
the pin already equals the tip distro-omarchy serves (advanced hours earlier by
opencharly/charly#1144): an archived repo never moves again, so charly's currency control
keeps reading `cur` instead of going red. Retiring the import there, or unarchiving the repo,
is the operator's call — which is why that leg is an ISSUE and not a silent deletion here.

**The census row was BLOCKING, and the first design run is why it is in this commit.** A
capability row's EVIDENCE is a citation the design gate RESOLVES AT ITS PIN; unpinning
`plugin-omarchy` turned `dulling.tsv:verb:omarchy` into a citation that identifies no file, and
`design-cite` failed on exactly that (`design-cite: FAILED (1 finding(s))`, quoted under "How
tested"). The row's own disposition already read `accept retirement` / `retired (A.9)`, so
retiring the row IS the disposition being carried out — and a fix that merely dropped the
citation would have left the census advertising a capability whose plugin no longer exists.

`TODO.md`, `DESIGN.md` and two `.agents/` skills still mention omarchy; not one of them is a
claim about the pin set, and the R5 bullet argues each.
