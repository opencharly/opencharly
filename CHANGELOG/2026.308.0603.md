# 2026.308.0603 — feat(design): let a skill name its owner and a hook its condition (batch 9)

## Summary

Batch 9 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). Two documentation
kinds had lost a field each:

- **`#Skill` had no `owner:`**, so the `ai.opencharly.skill` label could no longer be scoped to the
  candy whose composed chain carries the skill (`CollectSkills` filters by owner). It has one now.
- **`#Hook` had no `when:`** — the extra `settings.json` condition the emitted hook carries — **and no
  `mode:`** — the mode of the file it is emitted as. Both are in.

`kind:skill` becomes **`sharp`**. `kind:hook` **stays dulled** on what neither field covers: a hook
that emits a script **without** wiring it into the harness's settings has no form of its own.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN.cue` | `#Skill.owner`, `#Hook.when` and `#Hook.mode` |
| `DESIGN.md` | the typed-kinds example names a skill's owner and gives a hook a condition and a mode |
| `design/dulling.tsv` | `kind:skill` becomes sharp; `kind:hook` keeps only its aux-script gap |
