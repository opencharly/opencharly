## Summary

Head `05e8a0afb5aa43c287d00b4110c9f96c34144862` — 2 files changed, 7 insertions(+), 6 deletions(-).

The umbrella's own project manifest still declared the inner build-concurrency knob under its
RETIRED spelling, `podman_jobs` / `podman_jobs_cap`, after the contract renamed that knob to
`stage_parallelism` (`spec/schema/box.cue`, in opencharly/spec). The umbrella's build prose
still named the retired CLI flag `--podman-jobs` beside `--jobs`.

The retired spelling was not merely stale: it was INERT. `spec.Config.Defaults` is a
`spec.BoxConfig` — an alias of `spec.Box` — and it is decoded NON-strictly, so a key the
contract no longer defines is dropped silently; the block is still validated against the OPEN
`#BoxConfig`, so every gate stays green on a manifest whose tunables nobody reads. The
umbrella's build-speed tunables had therefore been configured in appearance only.

The prose now names `--stage-parallelism`, the flag the org's product parses as of the landed
producer (opencharly/charly#1082, tag `v2026.301.1632`). This repo's own pinned `charly` is
one wave behind and still parses the retired flag; its pin advance is this wave's next leg,
and the difference is measured under **How tested** (below).

### The RCA

**Mechanism.** `spec.Config.Defaults` is `spec.BoxConfig` (`spec/config.go:36`), and
`spec.BoxConfig` is an alias of `spec.Box` (`spec/charly_names.go:24`). The decoder is
non-strict, so a retired key is dropped rather than reported, and the block is validated
against the open type — a manifest can declare a knob that does not exist and every gate
stays green.

**Missed control.** No gate rejects an UNDECLARED key under `defaults:`, and no gate
compares a manifest's declared knobs against the contract's own set. The static proof is
pasted under **How tested** (below): the renamed key is refused BY NAME, the retired key is
accepted in silence.

**Blast radius.** Every manifest in the org that kept the retired spelling is inert the
same way. This change fixes the umbrella's instance; the class-level control belongs to the
contract's own repo and is filed below.

**Root fix.** Here: the umbrella's manifest declares the contract's keys, its cap becomes
the value the builder actually runs at, and its prose names the flag the CLI parses. In the
contract's repo: the decoder must NAME an undeclared key and point at the nearest declared
one (opencharly/spec#241).

### Why the cap becomes 4, and not 8

The podman-era cap of 8 does not carry over. The renamed knob resolves to the BuildKit
daemon's `[worker.oci] max-parallelism`, which is host-global per uid and set at daemon
START. Three independent authorities say 4: the SDK's own fallback
(`sdk/buildkit/build_helpers.go`, `StageParallelismCapFallback = 4`), the owning repo's own
landed manifest at the served tip, and the live daemon on this host (`max-parallelism = 4`).
The discriminator under **How tested** (below) shows what the alternative costs: at 8 the
build is refused AT the builder check.
