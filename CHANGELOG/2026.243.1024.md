**On `scripts/verify-pins.sh`, honestly.** An earlier version of this body said that
gate "runs on this PR". It does not — it runs in no workflow, only on demand via
`task verify` in a local checkout — and A1 was right to block the claim. It is also
not worth running here: its last check is
`git -C charly submodule status --recursive | grep '^[+-]'`, where `-` means
UNINITIALISED, and this job checks out submodules non-recursively. It can therefore
only fail; initialising them would check each out AT its gitlink and make it pass by
construction. Tried, measured, and left out rather than faked.

The pins in this diff are still verifiable, by the thing that actually gates them:
each is a real commit on the owning repo's default branch, and `task verify` on a
full local checkout is where policy B is enforced end to end.
