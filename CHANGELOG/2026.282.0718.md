## Summary

`AGENTS.md` is restructured around the ground-truth rules (opencharly/opencharly#446). It lands **now**, ahead of the gitlink bump, so the rule table the producer legs already cite is the one in force. The gitlinks advance in a separate `charly task sync` PR once every producer has merged.

**AGENTS.md**
- **R0–R10 are the spine.** They come right after R0, every other section opens with `Enforces: R…`, and "Any R-rule violation forbids the push".
- **Renumbered to a contiguous R1–R10:**
  - R4a → R4 and R7a → R8;
  - old R3/R4 → R2/R3;
  - old R2 (finish the whole cutover) moves into "Hard Cutover by Default", together with the Cutover Sizing Law and batching as **sizing only**;
  - old R8 (emitted artifacts) moves into R7, which also takes "concurrency is proven under load" from Key Rules.

  A one-paragraph "Old citations" note maps the old numbers for history readers.
- **R1 — fix it or file it.** Blocking findings (introduced here, a red or warning gate, or a claim that is false without the fix; unsure → blocking) are fixed in the change. Non-blocking findings are FILED, an existing issue or ONE new one, and listed under `## Filed issues`. A finding raised on the PR is fixed or rebutted, never filed away.
- **New "What the validator checks".** One row per validator check id (T1–T4, A1–A3, R0–R10, HC, PIL, CONC, ARCH, DISP, QUAL, COORD), each mapped to its rule and its owning skill section. The validator enforces and never instructs, and a BLOCK on an unstated obligation is a rulebook defect.
- **New "Commit, push, land", with "Before every update push: read everything, fix everything".**
  - Local commits are free, and R10 runs on the committed tree.
  - The first push opens a ready PR (no drafts), and one appended commit lands per review round.
  - Before each update push: read every comment and the full verdict in full, fix every finding, and give every validation failure a fresh RCA that names the missed control.
  - Answer coordination first.
  - The tier table says "never pushed" instead of "do not commit".
- **Part II #11 "Keep other sessions informed".**
  - Look before you start and before every push.
  - Post `STATUS` at every milestone.
  - Post `BLOCKS`/`UNBLOCKS` on both threads.
  - Answer coordination first.
- **Part II #7.** The takeover window is a floor that an owner's answer resets. The auto-close threshold is the repo's variable (`opencharly/charly` sets 20). Supersede touches four surfaces.
- **Duplication removed (R2).**
  - The Key Rules section goes; its two unique facts move to SDD and R7.
  - Part II #8 becomes a pointer to the Part I gate.
  - Post-Execution Policies folds into "Commit, push, land".
  - The monitor paragraph shrinks to its gist.
  - The dispatcher row for "claim-or-track an issue" moves to git-workflow.

**Umbrella-wide**
- Every rule citation in the hooks, harness files, scripts and `charly.yml` follows the renumbering. `reasonix.toml` points at "Commit, push, land", and `scripts/sync-pr-body.sh` / `check-pr-body-gate.mjs` use the new rule list.
- **`.pi/extensions/charly-gates.ts` no longer carries a hand-copied rules block**, which had drifted to the retired numbering. Every turn it injects the R1–R10, "Commit, push, land" and attribution sections READ from `AGENTS.md`. `scripts/check-pi-gates.mjs` fires that, and has a mutation that goes red when a section is renamed away.
