# 2026.329.1700 — docs(agents): continuous operation — re-arm the watcher and continue; ask only after RCA + spike

## Summary

The rulebook had no rule against the failure mode it kept producing: a session acts on a wake, writes
a status report, and stops, leaving actionable work undone until the operator prompts it again. This
adds that rule.

**Continuous operation** (Part I) — at every wake (a watcher's verdict, a merge or close, a check
run, a delegated result) the session acts on the result and arms the next wait. A report is never the
end of a task, and a session does not idle while actionable work remains. Setting the watcher before
a wait and re-arming it after a wake is **MANDATORY**. The only stop is the harness's
operator-question tool (`AskUserQuestion` in Claude Code), and only for a blocking question the
automatic RCA (R1) + spike (R12) loop could not settle; a free-text question, a report, or silence is
never the substitute, and the goal budget's ~80% stop is itself such a question.

Four edit sites, one rule:
- the new Part I section `## Continuous operation`;
- Part II **#12** for umbrella sessions, citing it;
- the watcher sentence in `## Agents, Workflows & Teams` now says re-arming and continuing are
  **MANDATORY**;
- a **Continuity** item in the Acceptance checklist.

The section states explicitly that it is a session-behaviour rule, not a diff property, so no
validator check observes it; the operator's loop is its control. That is deliberate — a check that
claimed to verify it could not.
