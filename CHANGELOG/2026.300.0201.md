## Summary

Head `5dd06e994c5fd331b1186f105ac8ce12ec09372a` — the round-1 fix of the four findings the validator raised on this pull request. The diff is 6 files changed, 991 insertions(+), 20 deletions(-) against `origin/main`.

**The diffstat a body states was READER-DEPENDENT, and now it is not.** The old head's diff carried a MOVED block: the reconciler's cap reader was reshaped in the first commit, and the Myers family aligned the move as three hunks while the histogram family aligned it as one. The line count a body stated was therefore decided by the reader's `diff.algorithm` — 574/26 under myers, patience and minimal, and 577/29 under histogram. The six file headers summed to 574/26, the body said 577/29, and nothing in the tree noticed. Two changes fix it. The diff itself no longer carries a moved block, so every reader computes the same count (991/20 four ways), and the reconciler GAINS an arm that compares the diff's line count under every diff algorithm — a diff whose count depends on the reader is now refused by the gate instead of published by a body.

**The cap gate was not hermetic.** Its three reader firings inherited the caller's environment, so an ambient `GITHUB_BODY_MAX` made the JS reader report the ambient number instead of the cap file's, and the refusal arms never read the mutated cap at all. Every reader is now fired with the ambient REMOVED, and the self-test runs the WHOLE gate under an ambient override and requires it green — with a mutation that removes the removal and turns that case red.

**The self-test counted mutations, not arms.** It said "4/4 arms live" while the gate has seven arms, and three of them watched nothing. Every arm now has a mutation that turns THAT arm red, and the self-test names the arms it covers and fails when one has none: "7/7 arms live, 1/1 hermeticity case live".

**The retired-wording claim contradicted the gate that names the wording it forbids.** The claim now states the exception the scan makes, and the gate's own verdict says the same.
