## What it brings in

    sdk#201            artifact_contains_text - a fifth shared artifact validator, so wl, cdp,
                       vnc, libvirt and spice screenshots all gain OCR at once
    charly#512         the host OCR engine and language pack, declared OPTIONAL per format
    plugin-wl#10       the schema field exposing it, and the sdk repin carrying the validator
    distro-omarchy#13  the eight assertions themselves
    charly#514         the box/omarchy gitlink and import pin

This is the assertion the desktop bed could not make before. A layer can be present, correctly
sized and on screen while drawing nothing: hypr-layers reports it, artifact_min_bytes passes on
a blank PNG, artifact_not_uniform passes on any wallpaper behind it. OCR separates a surface
that MAPPED from a surface that RENDERED - the weather panel really showing its pinned city,
the menu really showing its entries, a notification really showing its body.

The property the validator lives by is that failing to RUN is not failing to MATCH. With no
language data tesseract writes to stderr and exits with empty stdout, which is exactly what a
genuine no-match looks like; that mistake was made for real while building it, and the
validator now names the cause instead of reporting the text as absent.
