# 2026.324.1903 — docs(design): add the charly-only XDG overrides

## Summary

D-STORE-1 now names the **charly-only override for every XDG input** — `CHARLY_CONFIG_HOME`,
`CHARLY_CONFIG_DIRS`, `CHARLY_DATA_HOME`, `CHARLY_DATA_DIRS`, `CHARLY_STATE_HOME`,
`CHARLY_CACHE_HOME` and `CHARLY_RUNTIME_DIR` — and defines their contract:

- They are **absolute, COMPLETE charly paths**, not XDG bases: the `_HOME`/`_DIR` form names a
  charly root, and the `_DIRS` form names a colon-separated list of complete charly directories.
- They **take precedence for charly** over their XDG input; an empty or relative value is
  invalid and ignored, so the XDG default applies.
- `CHARLY_CACHE_HOME` wins when both cache overrides are set. **No CHARLY_ variable is dropped**:
  `CHARLY_CACHE_DIR` remains the compatibility cache override (used verbatim), and
  `CHARLY_REPO_CACHE` keeps its path-naming compatibility sentence (operator direction,
  DESIGN #1211).

D-STORE-11 now confines `HOME`/`XDG_*`/**`CHARLY_*`** reads to the resolver, and R11 names the
`CHARLY_*` category. The implementation wave — `spec/storeroot`, then `sdk`, then `charly` —
follows under opencharly/opencharly#1207.
