# 2026.309.0512 — feat(design): give the agent drivers their form and access, and name the rule (batch 31)

## Summary

Batch 31 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). Three driver rows were
waiting on the driver table, and they turned out to need **two different answers**.

**What was missing is filled.** `pi` and `tmux` carried no `form` and no `access`, so an agent session's
image form and its `exec`/`console` access had no row value at all. Both rows now declare them, and `pi`
gains `tool: "pi"` — a driver names the tool it drives, and that row had none.

**What the rule decides stays decided.** D-DRV-1 says *a driver is a row, never a plugin, kind, field or
command of its own* — so the runtime's typed channel operations and the six-operation engine envelope are
not fields a driver row can carry. That trade is now recorded in A.9, and the three rows take
`accept retirement` citing it, instead of asking for an amendment the rule forbids.
