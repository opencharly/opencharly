# 2026.308.0312 — feat(design): restore the protocol verbs' authored operations (batch 5)

## Summary

Batch 5 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). The four protocol
checks carried only the arms their first draft needed: `cdp` had two against 22 authored operations,
`mcp` two against 7, `appium` two against 48, `dbus` two against 4. **No example used any of them** —
the first example to cover these verbs is the one this change adds.

Restored from the operations authored documents actually dispatch:

- **`cdp`** — `status`, `tab` (the tab list), `open` (navigate), `session` (the screencast recorder's
  start/stop/status, with `fps` and `tab`);
- **`mcp`** — `ping`, and `tool` now takes an **optional name**: absent lists the server's tools,
  present asserts one exists (D-NAME-1 — a list-valued observation keeps its singular key);
- **`appium`** — `session` (create/delete with `caps`), `activity` (`app_id` and `activity`),
  `element` (`strategy` and `selector`, the find operation);
- **`dbus`** — `name` likewise optional, so listing the bus's names is expressible.

Two word sets became vocabulary tables (`_sessionAction`, `_appiumSessionAction`) rather than inline
enums (S3), and two keys joined D-NAME-1 (`caps`, `fps`).

| file | what changed |

| `DESIGN.cue` | eight arms and their definitions, two vocabulary tables, two D-NAME-1 keys |
| `DESIGN.md` | the new `protocol-verbs` example asserts every restored arm |
