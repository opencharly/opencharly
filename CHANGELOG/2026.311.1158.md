# 2026.311.1158 — feat(design): a machine body may carry what its bound driver understands (batch 58)

## Summary

Batch 58 of the DESIGN amendments (tracked in #838), implementing the **operator ruling on this goal**:
**D-HW-5 gains one exception, and it is S10's.**

A `pod` or `vm` body may carry **`config:`** — the one open field, whose fields the **owning driver's
plugin** defines and this document never types. `#MachineFields` gains it, so **pod and vm stay identical**
(D-DEP-1) and no vm-only field is invented.

**The precedent is already in the document.** `#ManagerPackage` is `{config?: #Opaque}` under S10, and A.9's
own row states the resolution: a manager's settings are *the plugin's keys inside `config`, so the design
neither names nor type-checks them*. A driver row is the closest analogue a manager has — both are owned by a
plugin, both are chosen outside the document, both would otherwise drag a foreign vocabulary into the authored
keys.

**Two consequences are stated inside the rule, not left for a reviewer to find:**

1. because D-DRV-2 keeps the driver *out* of the document, `config` is validated against the **bound** driver
   row's schema at **preflight**, not at authoring time as D-IR-6 otherwise requires;
2. a document that carries `config` is portable only across drivers **whose rows declare a config schema**,
   which narrows D-DRV-2's *"a box runs unchanged under every driver of its kind"* to payload-free documents.

`kind:vm` becomes **sharp**. `kind:firecracker` and `kind:kubevirt` **stay dulled** — their remaining gaps are
their own (the firecracker host surface is user configuration; the KubeVirt source arms and affinities are not
written), so each gets a separate ruling rather than riding on this one.
