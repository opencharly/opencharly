# 2026.332.0200 — docs(narrative): drop Android from VISION.md and GRIEVANCES.md after the #1318 retirement

## Summary

`VISION.md` tenet 2, its "Widen what one recipe can become" bullet, and the `GRIEVANCES.md` deployment-kind paragraph still described Android as a deployment mold. opencharly/opencharly#1318 removed `kind:android`, `deploy:android`, `verb:adb`, `verb:appium` and `step:apk-install` (`DESIGN.md:3080-3086`, operator decision 2026-11-27), and the docs generator publishes both root narratives verbatim, so opencharly.ai advertised a substrate that no longer exists.

Every sentence stays grammatical, and no clause loses its point: "one recipe, many molds" and "the long arc is *more molds under one wrapper*" both survive.

Two files change: `VISION.md` (tenet 2 and the heading bullet) and `GRIEVANCES.md` (one paragraph, reflowed after the deletion).
