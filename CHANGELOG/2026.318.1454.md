Units 4–7's envelopes (`#ResolvedLocal`, `#ResolvedKubeVirt`) are FLAT — strings plus `raw` — because their bodies had no typed envelope citations. Moving `#Vm` and leaving the envelope would leave the contract citing a def in another module.

**The split the measurement implies** (the `#PodSidecar` keeper precedent, scaled): the contract KEEPS `#ResolvedVm` and every def it cites; the plugin TAKES `#Vm` itself and the defs no envelope cites (`#VmInstaller`, `#VmChecksum`, `#VmKeyInjection`, `#VmCharlyInstall`), citing the keepers as `schema.#…`. The 48-def vocabulary tail splits the same way — the domain family's words stay with the contract, the body's move — and the exact word set is the producer leg's own measurement, not a figure this record asserts.

**Not started, deliberately.** The producer leg needs this decision first; the next round builds `schema/vm.cue` from the non-keeper list and proves it with the two-arm corpus sweep, exactly as units 4–7 did.
