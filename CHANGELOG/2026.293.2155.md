# 2026.293.2155 — docs(hc): point the Hard Cutover section at all three scope axes

## Summary

`Hard Cutover by Default` stated only the **Cutover Sizing Law**. The three axes that actually govern scope live one level down in `/charly-internals:cutover-policy`, and **the section pointed at none of them** — so a validator reading only the enforced HC text had no DECOMPOSITION axis to apply.

**This closes `opencharly/opencharly#544`**, filed because a validator BLOCK named the gap itself:

> *"It defers work inside the cutover's own scope, justified by size/review-capacity… ask for a rulebook provision permitting a tracked multi-PR leg — HC's current text provides none."*

**The provision exists.** `/charly-internals:cutover-policy` carries all three axes under "The three axes — do not conflate them":

| axis | governs |
|---|---|
| **BATCHING** | MANY separate small changes → collect into one theme-coherent cutover |
| **DECOMPOSITION** | ONE contract too large to land in a single atomic pass → split into ordered units as **forward motion only** |
| **NO-SPLIT** | ONE change's own scope → never carved in two to avoid doing it all now |

**This adds one paragraph naming all three and pointing at the skill** for their full criteria and forbidden framings. It does not restate them (R2). The paragraph opens *"Three axes govern scope, and this section states the law they all serve"* - **the law, not an axis** - because calling DECOMPOSITION "the middle one" invites exactly the conflation the skill warns against.
