# 2026.318.2226 — docs(program): the schema-ownership RCA and the mechanism design

## Summary

Successor to #1032 (auto-closed at the verdict threshold; the document is unchanged and the body now scopes its claims to exactly what §6 proves).

The diagnosis behind the remaining schema cutovers, and the design that replaces per-kind "keeper" judgements with one wire discipline plus a checker.

**The root cause it records:** the cutover treated the wire's shape as a constraint on the kind instead of as part of the cutover. Every kind whose wire named an authored type stayed in `spec` and was recorded as a "keeper" — and the citation counts that justified each one were measurements of the very wire that created the situation. Ten entities are still in that state.

**The design** (five invariants, one acceptance test, one checker):

1. the wire carries bytes, never an authored body type;
2. the gate is DECLARED by the owning plugin;
3. the host resolves it from the COMPOSED schema, with no base fallback;
4. the gate is process-blind — `Threaded.KindGates` ships the served CUE source so the loader can compile it without a registry;
5. ownership is CHECKED, not judged — a checker that prints `KEEPER (with its witness)` / `MOVE (with its §3.1 owner)` / `NOT_JUDGED` per def, with a reverse census that fails a keeper which lost its last citer.

**The migration is ordered, and its first cutover is already merged:** `opencharly/spec#322` collapsed the load wire to one opaque carrier. The word moves follow once steps 2–4 land.

**DESIGN.md additions — no goal is reduced:** a normative row for the deployment entity (the §3.1 gap that let two measurements disagree), the `D-SCH-7` wire rule, and the ownership check in the §25 census. The document also records the corrections owed to `spec/schema/node.cue` (a stale `#Vm` claim) and the four dead `#Vm*Word` re-exports.
