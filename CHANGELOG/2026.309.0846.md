# 2026.309.0846 — docs(design): record that an interaction path belongs to the venue (batch 35)

## Summary

Batch 35 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). Two rows wanted an
**application's own vocabulary on a venue**:

- `verb:tunnel` — an arbitrary `method`/`provider`/`tunnel_name`, a hostname, ports, an `expect` list;
- `terminal:tmux` — snapshot, transcript, detach/reattach and signal operations.

**D-VENUE-1 already decides it**: a venue access is one of `exec`, `copy`, `log`, `console`, `display`,
`forward`, `power`, and no kind, driver or application has an interaction path of its own. A tunnel **is**
`forward`; a terminal channel **is** `console`; the operations a channel offers belong to the venue and are
shared by everything that reaches it that way.

A.9 records the trade, and both rows take `accept retirement`.
