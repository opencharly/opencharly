# 2026.311.0122 — feat(design): a screen judgement carries its image, its assertion and its model (batch 55)

## Summary

Batch 55 of the DESIGN amendments (tracked in #838). `verb:vision` asked a model about an image, and **6 of its
7 authored keys had no field**: the images and their mime type, the per-call model with its request body, and
the assertion about the reply.

`#HarnessStepFields` gains the five that were missing:

| field | what it carries |
|---|---|
| `artifact` | the image the harness is shown |
| `image` | further images beside it |
| `mime` | what the images are (a closed four-word set) |
| `expect` | the assertion, in the **one** matcher vocabulary (`#Matcher`) |
| `llm` | this call's model and request knobs |

`#Llm` is a **named struct** (`base_url`, `model`, `timeout`, `request`) rather than an inline block, because
**`verb:agent` wants the same block** — it will share this definition rather than grow a second spelling of it.

The judgement stays a `harness_check` whose `evidence` includes `display` — that is what the design already said
a screen judgement is. What it gains is **what it looks at and what it must satisfy**.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN.cue` | five fields on `#HarnessStepFields`, and the named `#Llm` |
| `DESIGN.md` | the login-screen example exercises all five |
| `design/dulling.tsv` | `verb:vision` becomes sharp |
| `design/dulling-disposition.md` | regenerated |
