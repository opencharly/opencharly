# 2026.318.2151 — docs: rename CONTAINERD-IMAGES.md to IMAGES.md and move REASONIX.md into design/

## Summary

The umbrella's containerd image contract takes the name it now describes, and the reasonix
harness binding moves out of the repo root into `design/` — with the injection that keeps it
loaded landed, gated and proven in the same change.

The move needs the hook. reasonix loads instruction files ONLY from the workspace root
(`REASONIX.md` / `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md`), so a binding that moves off the root is delivered
by nobody unless something prints it into the session. Moving it alone would have been a silent
capability loss with no symptom until an agent could not resolve a `/charly-<family>:<skill>`
reference, which is why the move and the injection are one change.

- `CONTAINERD-IMAGES.md` → `IMAGES.md`, with its own title line and the design-ledger
  citations that name it (`design/parity.tsv`, `design/dulling.tsv`).
- `REASONIX.md` → `design/REASONIX.md`, its root-location claims corrected; the `SessionStart`
  hook (`.reasonix/soul-inject.sh`, wired in `.reasonix/settings.json`) emits it after the
  `SOUL.md` identity, so reasonix still receives it once per session.
- `scripts/check-harness-config.mjs` gains **check 7b**, asserting BOTH halves — the binding
  exists at its declared home, and the hook `cat`s it — plus the `--self-test` mutations that
  prove each half goes RED. Without it the gate stayed green over a binding nobody delivered;
  a control that cannot fail on the change is not a control.
