This PR touches gitlinks only: no code, no schema, no prose.

**The one sentence that is the whole reason this PR exists in this shape: the first `currency` run is a PREREQUISITE of the sync, not its result.** The verb names the paths that must be checked out *before* `charly task sync` can advance them, because `bump` does `switch --detach` **inside** each stale checkout. So the reading is what plans the work, and this is the first time this instrument has been used to plan its own work.

**And a rule with two clauses, both measured here — they belong together:**

1. **A `currency` reading is not portable across worktrees.** The walk needs no checkouts to RUN; it needs them to JUDGE. A missing checkout yields `differs (no checkout to judge ancestry)`. Measured from both sides in this campaign: the umbrella root gave **16 of 429 with 2 `differs`**; a worktree with uninitialised submodules gave **31 of 429 with 24 `differs`** against the same pin state.
2. **A reading must name its CHECKOUT POPULATION as well as its head, and the correct order is INIT FIRST, THEN the before-number** — because a before-number taken before the init cannot be compared with the after-number the sync will require. They would be two instruments, not a pair.

**And the finding that makes clause 2 more than hygiene: `differs` does not merely fail to judge — it HIDES an available advance.** On this tree the first reading said `8 behind, 17 differs`. After initialising those same 17 paths and changing nothing else, the identical binary said **`25 behind, 0 differs`**. Same total (32), a completely different composition, and **17 of the 32 syncable rows — more than half the wave — were invisible to a reading that did not own their checkouts.** A sync planned from the first reading would have advanced 8 gitlinks and silently left 17 behind while reporting a clean gate.
