The census fails **three ways**, and the third is why it was chosen over an allowlist:

1. a file carrying a retired name with **no census row** — a new shim, including a new file;
2. a file carrying **more** sites than its row — the shim grew;
3. a census row whose site is **gone** — the census must be lowered, so it only ratchets down,
   and a baseline generated against the wrong tree or a stale pin **fails on its first run**
   instead of silently mis-freezing debt.
