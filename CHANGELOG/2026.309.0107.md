# 2026.309.0107 — feat(design): give a cluster its policy surface (batch 25)

## Summary

Batch 25 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). A `kubernetes:` cluster
template carried a whole **policy surface** — default namespace, admission policy, kubeconfig context,
storage and ingress defaults — and `#KubernetesFields` was `{node?}` alone.

The five fields with authored use are back:

| field | authored use |
|---|---|
| `storage` (`#ClusterStorage`) | 50 manifests |
| `ingress` (`#ClusterIngress`) | 12 |
| `kubeconfig_context` | 7 |
| `default_namespace` | 4 |
| `admission_policy` | 4 |

`image_default` and `pod_default` have **0 authored uses** and stay out — the row now says exactly that
instead of counting the whole surface as missing.

**`class` is a §2 Not term**, so a storage or ingress provisioner is named by its `name`. That is not a
cosmetic rename: the vocabulary rule exists so the design does not borrow a layering word for a
provisioner.
