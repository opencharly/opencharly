# 2026.280.1144 — chore(watch): sweep the harness surfaces left claiming the 60s poll floor

## Summary

The umbrella leg of the watcher-interval cutover. `opencharly/marketplace#426` raised the watcher
family's `POLL_FLOOR` and all three defaults to 300s; this change moves the surfaces in THIS repo
that still state or implement the 60s floor, so they do not go stale the moment it lands (R1/R5).

- `.opencode/lib/watch.ts` - `POLL_FLOOR = 60` becomes `300`. This is a **duplicate implementation**
  of the same policy (R3), and `DEFAULT_INTERVAL_S` derives from it, so the cadence and the clamp
  move together.
- `.opencode/instructions.md` - the documented constant, `POLL_FLOOR = 300`, "sub-300s".
- `scripts/check-opencode-coord.mjs` - the gate **pins** the policy, so it moves with it: its
  assertion matched the literal `/below the 60s floor/` (that message is built from the constant),
  and seven fixtures drove `coord_watch` with `interval: 60`, which the new floor correctly refuses;
  the "keeps a valid interval" clamp case used `120`, which the new floor clamps to 300. Both are
  fixed here.
- `.pi/extensions/watch.ts` - **a functional constant, not just a comment**: `const INTERVAL = "60"` is
  the interval handed to the floor-enforcing watcher, so with a 300s floor the pi watch could not arm
  at all (exit 5). Now `"300"`.
- `.reasonix/watch.items` and `.dsh/README.md` - the comments that quote the cadence and the floor.

`STALL_MIN` is untouched everywhere: it is the stall window in MINUTES, not the interval.

Refs opencharly/marketplace#425.
