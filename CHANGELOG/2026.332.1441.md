# 2026.332.1441 — fix(pins): make the wave controls and generators assert only what they can prove

## Summary

Three findings filed from `opencharly/opencharly#1348` were one class: **a control or a generator that
asserted more than it could prove.** Each is fixed here, each with the instrument that proves it, and
each one previously cost a review round on a different PR.

- **`scripts/design-cite.sh`** recognised only `:#Name`, `:N` and `:N-M`. A construct citation
  (`pod_cmd.go:CpCmd.Run`) matched no subject, fell through to the bare-path pass, and was judged as
  "the file exists" — so the form the capability ledger is migrating to was checked by nothing (measured below), and a
  hybrid left behind by a find-and-replace (`ConfigSetupCmd.Run-485`) reached the org validator instead
  of failing locally. It now resolves the declaration at the pin, refuses a construct carrying a range,
  and fails loudly on a language it cannot model (each direction proved in **How tested** below).
- **`.pi/settings.json`'s marketplace pin** was the third harness surface naming the corpus, and the
  only one nothing derived: `charly task ff` re-derived two, the harness-config gate judged three, so
  every wave reddened the gate after its own derivation step reported success — and the pin was
  hand-edited twice in one cutover. The tag reading is now ONE library with two consumers, and the
  derivation writes from it.
- **`scripts/ff-body.sh`** emitted "every non-`distro-*` pin **is** its default-branch HEAD", an
  invariant a wave cannot hold and the body's own `!` legend refutes in the same section. The validator
  read the contradiction as a regression and blocked. The contract is now stated as the wave satisfies
  it, and the race is COUNTED from the table the body ships.
- **`scripts/check-pin-verb-scope.mjs`** gains THREE `NON_CLAIM_MENTIONS` entries, with their reasons
  committed beside them, because the fixes above introduce files that name `charly task ff`:
  `scripts/lib/corpus-tag.mjs` (names the wave as the CONSUMER of the shared reading), and
  `scripts/check-harness-config.mjs` and `scripts/sync-skill-bindings.selftest.mjs` (both name it to say
  WHY the gate and the derivation must read one library, and which state the self-test's arms survive).
  Each is a non-claim: the subject is the coupling or the arm, never the verb's SCOPE — none of the three
  tells a reader what the verb advances or narrows it to a subset. The gate refuses an unclassified file,
  so this is the classification it demands, not an exemption from the scope rule.
