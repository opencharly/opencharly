### The exception's cost, stated here and not in a comment

1. **The skill and its subject are no longer edited, reviewed, or tagged together.** Part II #5's first bullet exists to prevent exactly that drift: a ruleset or workflow change lands in `opencharly/.github`, its skill update lands here, and **nothing mechanically links the two** — no shared tag, no shared gate, no shared reviewer. That is the price of the exception, and it is real.
2. **The umbrella's gate set now polices prose it did not author.** The dated-figure fix above is the first instance, not the last: these two bodies were written under the carrier's lighter gate, and the umbrella's measurements/lint controls now apply to them.
3. **The umbrella becomes the home of record.** If `opencharly/.github` ever becomes a charly project (a manifest plus an `.agents/` tree), the correct move is a SECOND relocation of both skills — the exception's own reason expires the day that repo can author a skill.
4. **The corpus source grows and must move in step.** The umbrella pin has to carry these two skills before the carrier's ref may be dropped; dropping the ref first, or advancing the carrier past its drain, is the silent drop this migration exists to prevent (`#611`: `.1907` → 11 skills, `.2123` → 5, both `rc=0` with no message).
