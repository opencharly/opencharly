# 2026.309.2001 — docs(design): a manager's settings live in its config, by design (batch 47)

## Summary

Batch 47 of the DESIGN amendments (tracked in #838). `helm-release:` takes repo, chart, release, namespace,
wait and timeout, and the ledger called **five of them missing** because the package key is one
`<manager>:<token>` and the entry carries only the manager's `config`.

**They are not missing.** `#ManagerPackage` is `{config?: #Opaque}`, and **S10** makes that Opaque *"the
ONE open struct"* precisely because a manager's fields are the **owning plugin's**. The five settings are the
helm plugin's keys inside `config` — the design does not name them and does not type-check them, and that is
the trade.

A.9 records it; the row takes `accept retirement`.
