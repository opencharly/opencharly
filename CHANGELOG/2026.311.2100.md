# 2026.311.2100 — docs(design): a re-tag and a seed are the store's and the host's, not the document's (batch 65)

## Summary

Batch 65 of the DESIGN amendments (tracked in #838). `charly vm bake|build|import|clone|cp-box|retag|seed` was a
family of image and volume operations.

**D-CLI-2 already decides most of it.** The writer list names what may write, and `vm clone` and `vm import` are
**on** it — a document writes a kind:vm declaration and nothing else. `bake`/`build` are `box build` and a
`source:` node; `import-container-disk` is a source too.

| seed | the **user configuration**'s data seeding |
