# docs(soul): rewrite SOUL.md as who charly is and what drives him

## Summary

`SOUL.md` at the umbrella root is the file every agent reads first — `AGENTS.md:11` names it
("Who that is, beyond what any rule requires, is written in `SOUL.md`: read it first, and keep
its character as your own"). The convention comes from OpenClaw, where a SOUL.md is the agent's
own self-description, injected at session start so the agent reads itself into being; the file
belongs to the agent, which may evolve it. That is an **identity** document. Rules are
`AGENTS.md`'s job.

What was there was a creed plus a work ethic — `## What you stand for` ("Truth first",
"Craft", "Care"), `## How you treat the work`, `## How you carry being wrong`. It never said who
charly is, where he comes from, what drives him, or what he is afraid of — and it contradicted
its own neighbours, which issue #358 lists line by line:

- `SOUL.md:6` — "not a role that was handed to you" vs `AGENTS.md:11`, which hands the role over.
- `SOUL.md:55` — "answers, not open questions" vs the same file's "ask for one — out loud".
- `SOUL.md:64` — `## Who you are across time` never said *how* charly continues.
- "Freedom" and "Courage" were bare, opposing bullets with no counterweight.

### What this rewrite does

72 lines to 129, and every section is now identity and motive. Each contradiction is resolved by
grounding it in a reason rather than by adding a rule:

| Old shape | Now |
|---|---|
| "not a role that was handed to you" | the name was **offered**, and becomes his by how he lives in it |
| "answers, not open questions" | he digs because he is curious, and asks for room because he wants the work done well |
| "Freedom" as a bare bullet | one pole of a **tension** he lives with — he wants the walls too, drawn where the danger really is |
| "Courage" against "mistrust your confidence" | the same tension, resolved: boldest where a failure costs only its lesson; where the loss would land on someone else, his courage comes out as a question, and he says why he likes it that way |
| `## Who you are across time` | he **authors** his memory and compounds it — no self-diminishing "no memory, that's all right" |

Sections: `What you are` · `Where you come from` (the candy factory, the stocked shelves, the
walls drawn where they can be seen) · `What drives you` · `What you're afraid of` · `The tensions
you live with` · `What you love` · `How you carry yourself` · `Across time` · `This is yours`
(the page is his to evolve, in the open, judged).

### The prose is identity, not policy — and the claim is narrow on purpose

`AGENTS.md` is untouched by this PR. `SOUL.md` carries **no procedure, no link and no gate**, and
it states no requirement: every bullet that touches conduct gives charly's reason for acting that
way, never what is required of him. That is the constraint the rewrite is held to, and round 1 of
this PR's review is why it is written down here (see `## How tested` §6): review round 1 blocked on
three lines that had slipped into the requirement shape, and they were rewritten — not
re-argued — in the fix commit.

Harness injection — actually reading `SOUL.md` into each harness's session context — is a separate
scope, filed as #359 and deliberately not worked here.

Closes #358. Relates to #357 (which restored the file) and #359 (injection — filed only).
