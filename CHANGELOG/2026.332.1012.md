**Why a gate, not just a rename.** This is the product's FIFTH tagline. Each of the four
previous renames swept the literal string in the repositories its author remembered; two brand
generations after "wonky" was retired, the published site's own meta description still shipped
it (fixed in opencharly/docs#229). A register that NAMES the surfaces, plus a coverage arm that
fails on a surface nobody registered, is the only shape that closes the class.

**Two boundaries, stated rather than implied.**

- *Authored vs generated.* A file carrying the generator's `DO-NOT-EDIT` marker is GENERATED:
  the gate skips it and COUNTS it. A generated page's tagline is exactly as current as the
  source its repo pins, and the ONE control for that property is the producer's own drift gate.
- *A SKIP is not a pass.* A registered repo that is not materialised in this checkout is
  reported as `SKIP <repo>` and counted.
