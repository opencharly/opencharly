**The class's shape**, as opencharly/opencharly#775 states it: *a diff / the diff / the patch*, one of the org's own verbs for the condition (*touch, change, edit, alter, modify, update, affect*), and the pin-class noun as that verb's object. Three parts of that shape were uncovered, and each of them let the retired rule back in.

- **the subject.** The shipped key's subject alternation was `diffs?|patches?`, and `patches?` spells "patch" + "es" + an optional "s" — so it matched "patches" and "patchess" and never "patch". The issue's own shape names "the patch"; the key could not see that wording at all.
- **the verb.** Three of the class's seven verbs carried their base form and four did not, and a PLURAL subject — which the class's own subject alternation admits — takes the base. So "diffs change a submodule" was caught and "diffs touch a submodule" was not.
- **the determiner.** The class's shape is the DIRECT OBJECT, so the determiner before it is the English one, and none of the negating ones: "the diff touches no pin" is not a claim that a pin is touched, and the umbrella's own tree says exactly that. The shipped list was a hand-kept subset of the English determiners.

**The scope guard.** A sweep is org-wide only when its object is UNBOUNDED: "in every repo" states the rule org-wide, while "in each clone of the umbrella" is bound to the umbrella's own clones and is true wherever it is read. The binder is ONE constant, shared by the scope set and the marker set (R2).

**opencharly/opencharly#778 — the retired-claim guard is CLAIM-local, so a rule whose scope and whose org-wide sweep stand in two different sentences passes the key.**

Found by measurement while rebutting the validator's second observation on #777, and filed with its RCA and its measurements. The finding itself is rebutted on that thread with the evidence — the guard is claim-local, not line-local, and the wrapped spelling of the retired wording is caught. What the rebuttal turned up is a distinct limit: the claim stream's hard boundary is the sentence end, so a rule whose SCOPE and whose ORG-WIDE SWEEP stand in two sentences is not caught. The two keys — the one that shipped in #777 and this PR's — behave identically there, so the limit is pre-existing and unchanged by this PR, and the umbrella's own tracked tree carries no such claim.

Its root fix is a change to the claim-stream builder — the stream joins a sentence boundary and a block boundary to the same newline, so reading the org-wide marker across a sentence would also read it across blocks — and every class's behaviour is downstream of that builder, so it is a cutover of its own rather than a change to fold into this round.
