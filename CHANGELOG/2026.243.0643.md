## How tested

- **The gate:** `scripts/verify-pins.sh` (`task verify`) — the full pinning gate,
  which fails closed on a dangling pin, a dirty submodule, or a policy-B violation.
  It runs on this PR.
- **Producer:** `scripts/sync-gitlinks.sh`, whose output is the diff above. The pins
  are resolved from each repo's actual default-branch HEAD over the network, never
  hand-written.
