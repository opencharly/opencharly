# 2026.305.1941 — fix(scripts): measure the sync body's warnings instead of asserting zero

## Summary

The pin-sync body generator made two claims about its own output that its output
can falsify, so both are replaced with measurements taken FROM that output.

1. **It asserted zero warnings.** `scripts/sync-pr-body.sh` emitted "the producer
   resolves each pin and names any repo it cannot resolve rather than guessing; no
   warning appears in the pasted output" — while the producer log it points at can
   carry `WARNING:` lines and `[fail]` steps. The generator now greps those counts
   out of the producer log (`PRODUCER_WARNINGS`, `PRODUCER_FAILED`) and states the
   counts, saying each is accounted for in the body. A warning is never asserted
   away as zero again.
2. **It called itself "model-free".** The same body carries a caller-supplied
   `Agent:`/`Assisted-by:` pair naming a provider and a model, so a body cannot be
   both. The identity lines stay — they are truthful for the run that supplies them —
   and the "model-free" claim is gone.

This fix is **recovered, not new**: it lived only on `feat/umbrella-pin-sync`
(PR #492, closed unmerged) and was picked up by this session's branch/worktree
cleanup. It was never superseded — `origin/main` still carries the old
"no warning appears in the pasted output" sentence and has no `PRODUCER_WARNINGS`
reference at all (checked: `git show origin/main:scripts/sync-pr-body.sh | grep -c PRODUCER_WARNINGS` → `0` at the base this branched from).
