**They are not body moves; they are IR cuts.** The census says so, with reasons:

```

#Box        spec  KEEPER  witness K3, K5, K1
```

K5 is decisive. The IR cites the authored shapes **as compiled Go types**:

```cue
// candymodel.cue — a spec-owned IR shape
service?: [...#CandyService] @go(Service)
hook?:     #CandyHook            @go(Hook,optional=nillable)
// boxmetadata.cue — also spec-owned
hook?: #CandyHook @go(Hook,optional=nillable)
```

`@go(Service)` generates a `[]CandyService` field. Moving `#CandyService` into a plugin does not move that field — it removes the type the field is built from.

**So the order inside each word is:** (1) the IR stops carrying the authored shape — an opaque body, exactly as the WIRE already does (I1, D-SCH-7); that is the largest piece and the reason unit 8 (`vm`) took a whole wave. (2) The body moves to the manifest-declared owner (`kind:candy` → `plugin-candy-kind`). (3) Consumers re-point, and the beds prove it.

That is `DESIGN.md` implemented, not reduced: §10.2 already owns the IR and I1 already says bytes. What remains is that the IR still carries **one** shape it should carry opaquely — and until it does, a plugin cannot own its body without the IR losing a type it needs.

**§7b also records a measured warning about the census.** Its 85 `MOVE`s are a SCREEN, not a verdict: four candidates were examined and all four were correctly placed — `#CandyServiceOverrides` is a sub-shape whose citer (`#CandyService`) is a keeper; `#KubeVirtDeploySnapshot` and `#FirecrackerDeploySnapshot` are each cited by `snapshot?: [...]` inside their own file; and `#KindCluster` is an EGRESS schema (plugin-egress's job by §3.1's check-command row, not the substrate's).
