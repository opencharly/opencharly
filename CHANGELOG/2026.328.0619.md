# 2026.328.0619 — feat(governance)!: issues are RCA-first, a filer owns its blockers, and a fitting existing issue is always claimed

## Summary

Closes opencharly/opencharly#1259.

This makes the org's issue discipline explicit and mechanically enforced, following the operator's rulings of 2026-11-24:

1. **An issue is the output of a finished RCA.** It is filed only after a full, verified RCA. Its body states verified facts (commands with their output, `file:line`, measurements) and the RCA's conclusion: mechanism, missed control, blast radius and root fix, plus a spike for any high-risk claim. A hypothesis is not an issue.
2. **Blocking and non-blocking issues stay apart.** An issue a session files for something blocking its *own* progress is claimed by that session at filing and fixed by it. A non-blocking finding is filed for a later session and left **unclaimed**.
3. **An existing issue that fits the work is ALWAYS claimed**, so no work is duplicated. If it has a live owner, the session coordinates on the thread first, then claims a named slice or takes over comment-first.
