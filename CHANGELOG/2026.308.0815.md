# 2026.308.0815 — feat(design): make the ledger's dispositions readable and self-checking (batch 12)

## Summary

Batch 12 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). The ledger is 264
rows of tab-separated fact; a reader who wants to know **what happens to a capability** should not
have to parse it.

`design/dulling-disposition.md` is that answer — one line per capability with its verdict, its action
and, in plain words, what happens: `kept whole`, `owes <the amendment>`, `retired (A.9)` or
`unsettled`.

It is **generated** by `scripts/design-disposition.sh` and never edited by hand. The ledger control
now regenerates it on every run and fails when the committed copy differs, so the table cannot
describe a state the ledger does not have — a stale row is a red gate, exactly as a stale citation
is. A fifteenth mutation plants a stale line and requires the failure.

Appendix A registers it: **A.1** lists the file and its generator, and **A.10** says what it is and
why.
