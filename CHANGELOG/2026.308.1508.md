# 2026.308.1508 — feat(design): let a connected deployment carry its kind's fields (batch 20)

## Summary

Batch 20 of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). **`#LocalFields` was
empty**, and the ledger said a local deployment lost its install gates. Filling the definition was
**not enough** — and finding out why was the batch.

A top-level `local:` node is placed by **connect**, and `_#Placed.connect` spread only `_#Body`, never
the kind's `create` fields. Anything added to `#LocalFields` stayed unreachable from a document.

**The mechanism first.** `#DeploymentKindRow` gains `connect_create` (default `false`); a kind that sets
it spreads its `create` fields into the connect placement too. A local deployment **installs onto the
host it reached**, so its install gates are not a create-only matter. The other connected kinds
(`kubernetes`, `android`) are unchanged — an earlier blanket spread made `android`'s required
`device`/`api_level` required on a *connected* node and broke its example, which is how the flag earned
its place.

**Then the fields.** `#LocalFields` carries `install_opt` (`with_service`, `allow_repo_change`,
`allow_root_task`, `skip_incompatible`, `verify`, `builder_image`) and `env`, both registered in
`_deployField`. `kind:local` becomes `sharp`; the deploy-local example sets an environment and three
gates.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN.cue` | `connect_create` on the kind row and honoured in `_#Placed.connect`; `#LocalFields` and `#LocalInstallOpt`; `_deployField` |
| `DESIGN.md` | the deploy-local example sets `env` and `install_opt` |
| `design/dulling.tsv` | `kind:local` becomes sharp |
| `design/dulling-disposition.md` | regenerated |
