# 2026.308.0208 — feat(design): restore the generic display arms (batch 4a)

## Summary

Batch 4a of the DESIGN amendments the capability ledger records (tracked in #838). The display
check's arm set held **two** operations — `screenshot` and `window` — while authored documents
dispatch the generic ones constantly: 33 screenshots, 33 key presses, 18 focuses, 12 types, 12
closes, 12 clipboards, 12 execs, 7 statuses and 6 minimizes across the tree.

**Restored: the twelve generic arms** — `status`, `screenshot`, `window`, `key`, `type`, `click`,
`mouse`, `focus`, `close`, `minimize`, `clipboard`, `exec` — with the fields the compositors' own
schemas carry: the four screenshot artifact assertions (`artifact`, `artifact_min_bytes`,
`artifact_min_dimensions`, `artifact_not_uniform`, `artifact_contains_text`), the pointer pair and
button, the named key, the typed text, the window target, the clipboard action and its payload, and
the exec command. Two new enumerations are **vocabulary tables** (`_displayButton`,
`_clipboardAction`) rather than inline word lists, and the three keys that end in `s` join
D-NAME-1's list (`focus`, `artifact_min_bytes`, `artifact_min_dimensions`).

**Round 1 of review found two R10 gaps, both fixed here.** `button` was constrained to the literal
word list instead of `or(...)`, so a `click` with an explicit button was rejected; and seven of the
twelve arms had no example. The button is now `or(_displayButtonWord)`, and the display example
asserts **all twelve** arms.

**What stays out is compositor- or vendor-specific by design**: `hypr-*`, `sway-*`, `overlay-*`,
`atspi`, `ocr`, `xprop`, and the Cua and KVM vendors' own surfaces. Batch 4b decides whether the
generic display check should admit those at all.

| file | what changed |
|---|---|
| `DESIGN.cue` | twelve arms and their eight definitions, two vocabulary tables, D-NAME-1 keys |
| `DESIGN.md` | D-NAME-1's list extended; the display example asserts focus, a guarded screenshot, a key press and a typed line |
| `design/dulling.tsv` | the five display rows state their exact remainder and the restored surface |
