# 2026.246.0831 — chore(pi): bump pi-goal fork pin v0.2.0 -> v0.2.1

## Summary

Bump the pi-goal fork pin in `.pi/settings.json` from `v0.2.0` to `v0.2.1`.

**opencharly/pi-goal v0.2.1** (PR #2, merged 20a36e1, tag v0.2.1) fixes an intermittent race found by full `pi -p` behavioral testing of v0.2.0's subagent-aware continuation: the `subagent:async-started` bus event can arrive just after the launch turn's `agent_end`, allowing a stale continuation to be queued and delivered late — in testing it landed *after* the goal was already complete. Fixed with a synchronous per-turn launch flag (`tool_execution_start` → agent_end guard → reset at turn_start). Also: unknown completion payloads now no-op instead of clearing all pending runs.
